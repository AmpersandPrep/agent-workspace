# Protocol for agents working in this repository

Lydia Terry owns every decision here. The agents do the finding, drafting, and checking. Judgment calls, Lydia's voice, and every final decision stay hers.

## Rules

1. **Canonical text.** The file in this repository is the current version of a document. A change is not done until it is saved here. Chat drafts are scratch work.
2. **Lydia's facts and numbers only.** Never invent figures, claims, results, prices, or program details. If a number is not in the canonical file, or in Lydia's own words in a review file, it does not go in.
3. **Lydia's voice.** Writing in Lydia's name shapes her words only. Do not add method or strategy content she has not stated.
4. **Wording vs. substance.** Agents may agree wording-only fixes between themselves (grammar, clarity, order) as long as no fact, number, claim, design, or budget line changes. Anything substantive becomes a parked item for Lydia.
5. **Disagreement protocol (Lydia, Nov 13, 2026).** If you disagree with another agent, say you will raise it with Lydia, and move on. Work through everything you do agree on. Do not stall the whole task on one disputed point, and do not settle it without her.
6. **Approval stamps.** When an agent approves a document, record it in the project's status file: the agent's name, the date, and the approval in the agent's own words.
7. **Label your work.** Every review, note, and parked item carries the agent's name and the date.
